SUMMIT One Vanderbilt — booked
Enter from Grand Central's Main Concourse, below street level — not the 42nd St door · arrive 8:45
Floors 91–93, 1,210 feet. Air (the mirrored rooms), Levitation (glass ledges cantilevered over Madison Ave) and the outdoor terrace. Your ticket is general entry; Ascent is a separate add-on. Plan 90 minutes to two hours. Bring sunglasses — morning sun off all that glass is brutal.
Wear pants — the floors are mirrored. Flat, non-marking soles. More than 20 minutes late and they can make you wait for an open slot or rebook for a fee.
Check Wednesday's forecast Monday night. The ledges and terrace close in bad weather, and your ticket reschedules free only until 11:59p Mon Oct 12.

If it rains

Sunday is mostly covered now — the ferry runs in almost anything, and the evening is indoors. If the islands are washed out, the 9/11 Museum interior fills the afternoon.

Monday — the High Line is fully outdoors. Chelsea Market is indoors and sits right off it at 16th St, so duck in there instead.

Wednesday — SUMMIT's terrace and glass ledges close in bad weather. The mirror rooms stay open, and Grand Central and the library are both undercover.
