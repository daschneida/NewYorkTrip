9:00a
SUMMIT One Vanderbilt — first entry
45 East 42nd Street · 8 min walk · arrive 8:50
Floors 91–93, 1,210 feet. Air (the mirrored rooms), Levitation (glass ledges cantilevered over Madison Ave), the outdoor terrace, and Ascent if you booked it. Plan on 90 minutes to two hours.
Wear pants — the floors are mirrored. Flat, non-marking soles required. No heels, no heavy boots. And the glass ledges and terrace close in bad weather, with no backup day.
10:45a
Grand Central Terminal — straight downstairs
The whispering gallery is the tiled arch outside the Oyster Bar: stand in opposite corners facing the wall and talk normally. The celestial ceiling is painted backwards — mirror-reversed against the actual sky. Nobody has ever fully settled why.

If it rains

Wednesday morning, SUMMIT's outdoor terrace and glass ledges close in bad weather. The indoor mirror rooms stay open, and Grand Central and the library are both undercover.
